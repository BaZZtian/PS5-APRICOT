Corresponding app source and build entry point
  The source package accompanying the same release contains the player,
  compiler, original shaders, required headers and linking scripts. Invoke
  platform/ps5/tools/build-game-ps5.sh after building the exact dependencies
  above. ORQENVA_VULKAN_ROOT selects the PS5_Vulkan source/build directory;
  PS5_CLANG and GLSLANG may select the Clang18 and glslang executables.
  The upstream SDK and RADV archive hashes are checked by the app build.
  Rebuilding from source permits modifying and relinking the LGPL display
  component; no proprietary game module or developer fixture is required.
  Game-derived code/data must be prepared privately from the user's dump.
